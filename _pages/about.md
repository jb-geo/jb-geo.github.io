I am a geospatial analyst with experience building spatial data products, automating geoprocessing workflows, and developing web-based geospatial applications across environmental and infrastructure domains. My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, spatial analysis, and computational methods, with a growing focus on extracting insight from Earth observation data to better understand environmental and societal systems.

My current interests center on:
======
- Remote Sensing
- Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and Earth Observation
- Machine Learning for Spatial Analysis
- Geopolitics and Human–Environment Systems
- Computational Geospatial Methods

One theme that continues to shape my interests is understanding how spatial data can reveal patterns that are difficult to observe directly—whether through environmental monitoring, infrastructure systems, or broader questions involving human behavior and geographic change.

Outside of my professional experience, I have pursued independent remote sensing projects focused on developing stronger analytical and research skills. My featured work explores seasonal river ice dynamics using Sentinel-1 SAR imagery, combining Earth observation data, reproducible workflows, and quantitative interpretation to investigate environmental change through time.

I also draw influence from earlier graduate coursework completed in Stockholm, Sweden focused on sustainable urban planning and design. That experience expanded my interest in how spatial systems connect environmental processes, urban development, and geopolitical questions across different scales.

This site documents selected projects, methods, and technical writing as I continue building toward more advanced work in remote sensing and geospatial research.

Featued Project
======
### Monitoring Seasonal River Ice Dynamics Using Sentinel-1 SAR
Using Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar imagery and Python-based analysis workflows, this project investigates seasonal freeze–thaw dynamics and river ice evolution within interior Alaska. The project combines time series analysis, image processing, and interpretation of radar backscatter patterns to explore how remote sensing can support environmental monitoring in dynamic northern systems.
