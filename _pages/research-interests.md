I’m interested in the many different types of information that can be extracted from satellite observations and how that information can tell stories about the events shaping our planet. I have a unique desire to not only understand the technical concepts underpinning remote sensing technologies, but also complex interplay of human behavior, geography, and environmental processes. 

My current interests include remote sensing, machine learning, geocomputing, geospatial analysis, and geopolitics.
