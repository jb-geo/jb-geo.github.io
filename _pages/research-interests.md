## Remote Sensing

I'm interested in the types of information that can be extracted from satellite observations and I'm eager to learn more about how this information can better help us understand dynamic earth processes- particularly those that directly affect human life. I thoroughly enjoy producing visually appealing and informative maps, however, I am also interested in developing methods that can transform imagery into quantitative information that aid in decision making.
