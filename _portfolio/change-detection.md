## Project Overview

### Why NDVI?

NDVI measures vegetation vigor using the contrast between near-infrared reflectance and red reflectance.

Healthy vegetation strongly reflects NIR while absorbing red light, producing high NDVI values.

Comparing NDVI between years provides a simple yet effective indicator of vegetation gain or loss associated with urban expansion.

#### Interpretation

Positive ΔNDVI values indicate increased vegetation density between 2016 and 2024.

Negative values indicate vegetation loss.

Around the Port of Savannah, vegetation loss is expected near recently developed industrial infrastructure, while localized gains may occur in restored or unmanaged areas.
